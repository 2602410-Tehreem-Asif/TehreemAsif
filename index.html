Islamabad,The Beautiful

Islamabad, the capital city of Pakistan, is widely celebrated as one of the most scenic and well-planned urban centers in the world. Nestled peacefully against the lush green backdrop of the Margalla Hills, the city harmonizes modern architecture with abundant nature. Wide, tree-lined roads, neatly organized sectors, and expansive parks like Fatima Jinnah (F-9) Park give it a calm, clean, and refreshing atmosphere. Iconic landmarks such as the majestic Faisal Mosque and the Pakistan Monument stand as proud symbols of national identity, making Islamabad a truly captivating blend of tranquility, order, and natural beauty.
